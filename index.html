Approach

What sets us apart

Builders’ DNA
We architect products, assemble funding teams, and stay hands-on through launch and scale-up.
Patient Capital
With no outside investors, timelines follow the opportunity, not the fund cycle.
Focused Problem Solving
We look for narrow, high-impact pain points where domain expertise and technology can unlock outsized value.
Selective Scope
Fewer bets, higher conviction. Each venture must earn a place in our portfolio by proving it can become category-defining.
Global Perspective
While headquartered in Riyadh, our network and market each span major innovation hubs worldwide.

What we don’t do
